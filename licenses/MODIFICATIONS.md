# Checkpoint origins and modifications

**Built with Qwen.** The Qwen2.5 checkpoints derive from the corresponding Qwen base model. Training produced the study adapters and updated weights. The original experiment paths and saved bytes are preserved. The model weights, their configuration and the adapter files describe the research runs. They are distributed with the licence applying to the corresponding base model. The 3B materials are for non-commercial research or evaluation under the Qwen Research License Agreement.

The GPT-2 starting checkpoints contain pretrained OpenAI weights converted into the study implementation. The GPT-2 training runs produced adapters or updated weights. The GPT-2 licence is enclosed with those downloads.
